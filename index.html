Book café bingo

Browse the shelves, glance at covers and blurbs, and claim a square whenever a book fits. Sixteen squares, about five minutes a book.

90:00
A 90-minute round. Start the clock when you walk to the shelves.
Pick up your first book.

Scores

Tap a name to switch whose turn it is. Each square scores the points on its corner. A line is worth +5 and a full house +10.

Bonus dare

Fancy a side quest? Draw a dare for bonus points.

  • One book claims one square. Tap a claimed square again to undo it.
  • Glancing at the cover and blurb is enough. No need to read the whole book.
  • Hard mode: read the first page out loud before you claim a square.
  • When the clock hits zero, the highest score wins.
